Privacy Policy for TSStats

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

1. Overview

TSStats is a service that collects and displays aggregated statistics related to mod usage, authors, and game activity. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Collect

a. Usage Data

TSStats collects non-personal data related to application usage, including:

  • Installed mods
  • Mod identifiers and versions
  • Game identifiers
  • Usage frequency (e.g., launches, active sessions)

This data is used strictly for statistical and analytical purposes.

b. Device Identifier

TSStats generates a one-way encrypted device identifier to distinguish unique users.

  • This identifier is non-reversible and cannot be used to recover the original device information
  • It is used to count unique users and prevent duplicate or spam data
  • Users may view their own encrypted identifier, but it cannot be used to identify them personally

c. Technical and Network Data

Through infrastructure provided by Cloudflare, limited technical data may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Approximate geographic location (e.g., country or region level)
  • Request metadata (headers, timestamps, etc.)

This data is used for:

  • Security and abuse prevention
  • Performance optimization
  • Aggregated geographic insights

3. How We Use Information

Collected data is used to:

  • Generate aggregated statistics (e.g., popular mods, top authors, player counts)
  • Maintain and improve service performance
  • Prevent abuse and ensure data integrity

All publicly displayed data is aggregated and not personally identifiable.

4. Data Storage

TSStats currently uses Cloudflare KV storage to store collected data.

  • This storage system may change as the project evolves
  • Data may be migrated to other storage solutions in the future, with similar or improved privacy protections

5. Data Sharing

TSStats does not sell personal data.

However:

  • Aggregated, anonymized statistics may be publicly displayed
  • Data is processed through third-party infrastructure providers, including Cloudflare

6. Data Retention

  • Aggregated statistical data may be stored indefinitely for historical analysis
  • Technical logs and request-level data may be retained temporarily for security and debugging

7. Cookies and Tracking

TSStats does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Limited cookies or similar technologies may be used by Cloudflare for:

  • Security (e.g., bot protection)
  • Basic service functionality

8. User Control

  • TSStats does not require user accounts
  • Users may avoid participation by disabling or not using integrations that send data to TSStats
  • Users may access their encrypted device identifier if exposed by the client application

9. Security

Reasonable safeguards are implemented, including:

  • HTTPS encryption
  • Rate limiting and abuse detection
  • Secure infrastructure via Cloudflare

However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children’s Privacy

TSStats does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of TSStats after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. You will be notified when the privacy policy is updated.

12. Contact

For questions or data removal requests: takedown@ts.scyye.dev

Be sure to provide the user ID that is printed in the console when starting the game with the mod.
